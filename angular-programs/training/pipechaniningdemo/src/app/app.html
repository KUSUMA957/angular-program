🎯 Key Takeaways

  • Order Matters: trim | titlecase vs titlecase | trim
  • Performance: Pipes are pure functions - they only re-execute when inputs change
  • Readability: Chain pipes left-to-right for clear data transformation flow
  • Built-in Pipes: Angular provides many useful pipes out of the box
  • Custom Pipes: Create your own pipes for specific transformations (like our trim pipe)
